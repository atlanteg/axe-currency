You can now dismiss the number pad: a keyboard button appears above it, and tapping the background closes it too. On iOS the numeric keyboard has no built-in Done key, so the amount field kept it open and covered the lower rows.

Also in this release: tablet layout keeps the currency list at a readable width, English is the default language (your device language is available as an option), country flags for all fiat currencies, and drag-to-reorder via the ↑↓ button in the header.
